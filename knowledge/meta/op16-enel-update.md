# OP16 Update — analyse méta du coach (source abonnement)

> Contenu fourni par le coach pro (abonnement), intégré le 2026-07-19. Fait foi pour la méta OP16 côté Enel.

## 1. The new meta outlook

With the introduction of OP16, Enel now has to face some new and very interesting matchups. One of them is also one of the most difficult ones, which is BY Teach, while other new matchups are actually much easier to navigate.

In this chapter, we will explain which new decks have been introduced into the format, how the meta outlook looks for Enel in the current meta, and whether it still makes sense to play this deck in the OP16 format.

### New tierlist draft

*(image « new tierlist draft » non fournie — draft de joueurs japonais)*

As we can see from a first draft made by some Japanese players, the meta outlook is very similar to what we already had in OP15, but with the introduction of BY Teach as one of the worst matchups for Enel. At the same time, we also have other matchups like UG Luffy and PY Rosinante, which I think are going to be among the best contenders in this meta, together with a large number of Tier 2 decks that are probably not going to see a lot of play in the coming weeks.

What I would change about this tier list is putting PY Rosinante and UG Luffy in Tier S instead of Tier 1.5. To be honest, I don't really believe in Tier 1.5. I think there is just Tier 1 and Tier 2, and then I would put everything else in Tier 2, with Yamato probably being one of the very few decks that can actually withstand the current meta. For this reason, I will focus only on the five decks that we already mentioned, plus Yamato.

Enel positions itself very well in the current meta, still having a lot of good matchups into most of these decks, but at the same time suffering against the yellow counters like BY Teach and UY Nami, which are still among the worst matchups. Even then, Enel remains a solid deck that can have a real game plan against both of them, while easily winning against the other matchups.

### Enel matchup spread

*(image « Enel matchup spread » non fournie)*

As we can see from the picture here, Enel clearly has a very good spread into the six decks we already mentioned, while struggling against the yellow counters, which are BY Teach and UY Nami.

I think the matchup against UY Nami will become a little bit better depending on how they build the deck. I have seen a lot of Nami lists playing more Triggers and moving towards a more Sky Island package to survive the BY Teach matchup, so this matchup might become slightly better for us.

At the same time, we now have BY Teach as a new contender, and it is a very difficult one because of how the deck is built. It really counters our deck very well. I still think Enel can win against this new contender, and as you can also see from the spread against the other top decks, Enel is still in a very good position. For this reason, I think it is probably going to remain a Tier 1 deck.

In the next chapter, we will talk about the new build, which is focused on winning the yellow matchups while not changing too much from the OP15 approach. I still think Enel is one of the best decks in the format, and I recommend playing it if you are not fed up with the deck. At the same time, I believe a lot of people will shift towards the new decks, because whenever there is a new expansion, people naturally want to try the new things. This means Enel might decrease in popularity, but it will still remain one of the top-tier decks.

### Conclusion

To conclude this chapter, I believe the meta has shifted towards a new counter deck to Enel, but overall, I still think the environment is good enough for this deck to remain a top-tier choice.

Even if we now have to face a new bad matchup in BY Teach, which was not present in the previous meta, I think the matchup is going to be as hard as UY Nami, but still very winnable. Depending on the popularity of the different picks, we might even see a revamp of Enel towards the end of the meta, because BY Teach players might start losing to UG Luffy, which could then open the door for Enel to come back again.

For this reason, the position of Enel will depend a lot on how the meta shifts and on which decks become the most popular. If the format moves in the right direction, Enel can easily come back as one of the best picks.

## 2. The new decklist changes

For the new decklist that we are proposing today, nothing has really changed too much from the OP15 meta. I think the most important thing is that we adapt our decklist for the new bad matchups, especially BY Teach, so it becomes really important to have access to multiple copies of Gamma Knife.

I think there are very few things we actually need to change from the previous meta, because most of the new matchups we are going to face can basically be solved thanks to Gamma Knife. For example, increasing the number of Senior Pink is, in my opinion, one of the best things you can do to improve those matchups.

I also received a lot of questions about Holly and whether we should include it in the decklist, but I don't think it is that needed, to be honest. Probably the best matchup where Holly could be very useful is Black Yamato, but after my experience at the latest Regionals and Treasure Cups, I didn't see that many Black Yamato players, to be honest. For this reason, I would not include the dog in the decklist for now.

I think this decklist is very good into most of the popular decks in the current meta, such as UG Luffy, the mirror match, BY Teach, and UY Nami. If you want to increase your consistency against UY Nami, you can consider including Holly as well, but I think that would make your matchup against BY Teach worse, and probably the PY Rosinante matchup as well.

So it really depends on the field you are expecting in the current meta, and also on your playstyle. I don't think the dog is bad, but to be honest, I am not really a fan of it myself. I prefer going with a more controlling approach, especially to make my BY Teach matchup more manageable.

### New decklist for OP16

*(liste extraite dans `decks/enel.md`, image dans `decks/media/op16-enel-decklist.png`)*

Talking about the decklist, I think what we already covered is good enough. Everything you learned from OP15 can basically be translated to the new decklist. The general game plan of the deck stays the same, and there is not really a big change in what you want to achieve throughout the game.

You should always focus on the value side of the deck, and eventually add a lot more aggression in the matchups where you actually need to do that.

Looking at the new matchups, I don't think there is anything too crazy that we need to cover. I think the most complex one is going to be PY Rosinante, because it is purely based on DON!! management, which is something we did not have to focus on as much in the previous sets.

Having said that, I think if you were comfortable with the deck in OP15, you will still find it insanely good in the current format. Of course, as we already mentioned, the new yellow decks are a problem, but Enel still positions itself as one of the best decks in the meta.

Honestly, I think Bandai probably did not expect this deck to become such a strong control deck. Having access to cards like Gamma Knife, together with the generic purple package that basically gives you an answer to every threat in the game, is good enough to make this deck still one of the best picks.

As we already saw in the recent Regionals, the deck is insanely strong, with a big representation in top cuts, and it will probably remain one of the best contenders until the end of the OP16 meta.

### Conclusion

I will try to update the guide as much as possible as the meta develops, even though I don't think we will see any big surprises in the upcoming tournaments.

This meta will not be very long either, because we will basically have only one month of OP16 before the new STs come out. However, I believe we might still see some small changes depending on the field.

If the field stays the same as it is right now, with a big representation of yellow decks, Enel, and UG Luffy, I think this decklist will probably be one of the best choices.
